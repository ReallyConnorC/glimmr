Glimmr Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Glimmr is a homeschooling planner for parents. This page explains, in plain English, what information we keep and why.

What we store

  • Parent account: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Learners: each child's first name, birthday and chosen avatar. The birthday is only used to work out their age and suggest suitable work.
  • Timetables and activities you create, including any links and instructions you type.
  • Results: which activities were finished and quiz scores.
  • Login codes: a random code for each child's QR login. It contains no personal information.

We don't collect children's email addresses, photos, locations or contact details, and children never create accounts or passwords.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a secure database provider. Each parent can only access their own family's data. A child's QR code only lets that device see that one child's timetable and save that child's results.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't show adverts or use tracking cookies.
  • Your device remembers that you're logged in, and which child a device belongs to. That's all it stores.

Videos and links

Some activities link to YouTube. YouTube is run by Google and has its own privacy policy. Videos added by parents play using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Please supervise children when they use outside websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Profile → Delete all my data. This permanently erases your learners, timetables, results and login codes. If you just want to stop a child's device from working, use Profile → QR → Make a new code.
